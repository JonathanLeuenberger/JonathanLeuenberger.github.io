Jonathan Leuenberger

I am an undergraduate researcher at New Mexico State University pursuing Ph.D. positions within the field of Information Science. I am advised by Dr. Shiva Darian, conducting research on how disenfranchised people engage with technology, such as our work with how refugees adapt to mobile technologies while seeking asylum at the US–Mexico Border. I am also working with Dr. Bill Hamilton on phishing scams in short-form video platforms.

Broadly, I am interested in community collaborative work and qualitative methods — exploring how technology intersects with culture, infrastructure, and marginalized communities. I am especially drawn to the role of data infrastructure and systems of control in shaping how people experience digital life.

Major: Anthropology  |  Expected Graduation: May 2027
